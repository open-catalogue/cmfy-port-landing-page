Conclusion

Malayalam YouTube comedy has grown into a dependable source of entertainment for viewers looking for a quick break from stress. From Karikku's ensemble-driven limited series and sitcom-style stories to short productions from SUB Originals and Alambanz, these shows offer familiar characters, lively performances, and relatable everyday humour.

Whether you prefer workplace comedy, family chaos, friendship stories, or experimental short films, this list provides an easy starting point. Every series above is available on YouTube, so you can explore the official episodes, discover the cast, and choose the style that suits your mood.
