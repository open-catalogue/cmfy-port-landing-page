About Cmfy Portal

Cmfy Portal is a web portal designed to help people discover creators, professionals, businesses, and interesting places near them. It focuses on independent creators, gaming centres, aesthetic food spots, small businesses, local stores, and other unique services.

The portal also provides digital product and service catalogues for businesses, helping them showcase their products and services online in a simple and well-organized way.

Cmfy Port primarily focuses on Edappal and nearby areas along the Malappuram–Thrissur border, while profiles from other locations can also be added to the portal.
